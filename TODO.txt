R11 rung under 12), finding-level hallucination filtering under 4, the request
timeout under 5, feature 6 in full (confirmation gates), the deploy and smoke

The ADK lane runs alongside that. Feature 12 is live and the flip is done: the
default engine has been "adk" since the 2026-08-30 gate run, with
SILKSCREEN_ENGINE=sdk as the kill switch, and the image, CI and setup all
install the adk extra. What remains under 12 is the queue behind the flip —
R9 and R11, then R13 through R16, with R17 and R18 as ideas. Feature 13 is
complete, D5 included (check_docs --fix ran once the counts settled).

Features 14 and 15 landed with PR #10 and changed what a run produces: a whole
KiCad project rather than one placed board file. What is left of them is the
web UI catching up (the SPA shows neither the schematic nor the tracks) and the
router's two honest limits, rip-up-and-retry and fine-pitch packages.

Worth knowing for anything touching the emitters: KiCad 8.0.9 is installed
locally and `kicad-cli sch erc` / `pcb drc` are now the strongest check the
project has. The first DRC ever run on an emitted board found a real short that
the whole test suite passed over. See "Verifying against real KiCad" in
CLAUDE.md. It is a local gate, not a CI one.
